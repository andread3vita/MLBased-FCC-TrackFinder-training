GATR pruning sweep (GATR_CIRCE_LOSS) via slurmTrain.sh
=======================================================

Purpose
-------
Prune the GATR model to reduce inference time per event while keeping the
baseline physics (tracking) performance. Two pruning axes are swept:

  - depth : NUM_BLOCKS            (baseline: 10)
  - width : HIDDEN_MV_CHANNELS    (baseline: 16)
            HIDDEN_S_CHANNELS     (baseline: 64)

Architecture compute/parameter count grows linearly in NUM_BLOCKS and
superlinearly in the hidden channel counts, so configs moving down either axis
shrink inference cost.

LR decision (START_LR / MIN_LR)
-------------------------------
Kept at the tuned baseline START_LR=4e-4, MIN_LR=1e-5 in ALL configs:
capacity is reduced, not increased, so no LR rescaling is required a priori
and results stay comparable to the baseline recipe. The values are repeated
explicitly in every command to keep each line self-contained.
If an aggressively pruned config (b2, b4/mv8/s32) underfits, rerun it with
START_LR=6e-4 as a follow-up.

Fixed everywhere: EMBED_DIM=4 (script default), same train/val globs,
4 GPUs (script default GPU_IDS). Each config has a distinct OUTPUT_DIR,
--wandb-displayname and --job-name matching its architecture.

Data: training uses the first 600 sorted matches of the train glob
(MAX_TRAIN_FILES=600 in every --export list); validation uses the full glob
(already capped at 100 batches/epoch by the script's LIMIT_VAL_BATCHES=100).
For smoke tests before queueing the full set, lower MAX_TRAIN_FILES (e.g. 5)

Configuration list
------------------
 # | blocks | hidden_mv | hidden_s | intent
---|--------|-----------|----------|--------------------------------
 1 |   8    |    16     |    64    | mild depth cut (80% of baseline)
 2 |   6    |    16     |    64    | moderate depth cut
 3 |   4    |    16     |    64    | strong depth cut
 4 |   2    |    16     |    64    | aggressive depth cut
 5 |  10    |     8     |    64    | mild width cut (MV axis)
 6 |  10    |    16     |    32    | mild width cut (S axis)
 7 |  10    |     8     |    32    | width cut on both axes
 8 |   8    |     8     |    32    | combined mild
 9 |   6    |     8     |    32    | combined moderate
10 |   4    |     8     |    32    | combined aggressive

Evaluation guidance
-------------------
Compare each run against the b10/mv16/s64 baseline on tracking
efficiency/fake rate (validation) and measure inference time per event of the
exported model. Pick the smallest config whose physics metrics match baseline
within tolerance; expected ordering of inference cost follows the table rows
1 -> 4 and 5 -> 10 from near-baseline to smallest.
